# Transparency Lab MVP

Zero-cost market test for a transparency-first comparison and commerce concept.

## Hypothesis

People and small businesses will ask for an independent second opinion when:
- supplier or contractor quotes are difficult to compare,
- surplus inventory may still have market value,
- comparison portals may not show the economically best result.

The MVP tests demand before building a full product.

## Public pilot offer

1. **Quote Benchmark** — normalize 2–5 offers and surface scope gaps, cost drivers and open questions.
2. **Surplus Value Check** — estimate whether surplus inventory is worth further market research and which disposal/sales path may fit.
3. **Benchmark the Benchmark** — independently cross-check comparison-portal results and explain material differences.

## Guardrails

- No technical approval of substitute parts.
- No insurance brokerage, legal, tax, investment or medical advice.
- No binding valuation.
- Public GitHub issues must contain **no confidential, personal or commercially sensitive documents**.
- Early pilot reports are AI-assisted and human-verified.

## Demand test

Primary signal: a visitor voluntarily requests a pilot without outbound sales.

Success criteria for the first test:
- at least 5 qualified inbound pilot requests, and
- at least 1 requester willing to pay for a follow-on analysis or transaction-support service.

Until that happens, no paid tooling and no complex backend.

## Deployment

The static landing page lives in the repository root and is designed for GitHub Pages.

To publish:
1. make this repository public,
2. enable GitHub Pages from the `main` branch, root folder,
3. verify the generated Pages URL,
4. share only the Pages URL, not personal contact details.

## Status

Built as a staging MVP. Public launch is intentionally blocked until the repository is made public and Pages is enabled.
